3D Parallelism: Composing the Axes onto Real Hardware

Pages 09–13 took one axis each. None of them is a choice against the others: TP × PP × DP is a factorisation of the GPU count, and every GPU ends up with three coordinates. What decides whether the configuration is fast is not the factorisation but where each factor lands in the network. This page composes the axes, maps them onto an eight-GPU NVLink node behind a 400 Gb/s fabric, and computes a 70B recipe term by term.
