Part 20

Sequence Parallelism

Tensor parallelism divides the weights and the MLP activations by the TP degree, and divides the LayerNorm, dropout and residual activations by one. Nothing in those regions mixes tokens, so they can be split along the sequence axis instead — the axis TP left free. The transitions cost an all-gather in and a reduce-scatter out, which is exactly the all-reduce tensor parallelism was already running. Same bytes on the wire, strictly less memory.
