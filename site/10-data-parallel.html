Part 10

Data Parallel: Split the Batch, Average the Gradients

Every GPU holds a complete copy of the model. The batch is what gets cut up. Each replica computes a gradient from its own samples — and those gradients are genuinely different from each other — then one all-reduce averages them. This page walks that step with real numbers and proves the averaged gradient is bit-for-bit the gradient a single GPU would have produced on the whole batch. DDP is not an approximation.
