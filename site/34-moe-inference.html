Part 34

MoE Inference

Training and inference are different problems for an MoE, not two views of one problem. In training a large batch hits every expert and grouping produces real GEMMs. In decode the batch is small and it scatters — each expert may see one or two tokens, and you still read its entire weight matrix to serve them. The sparsity that bought capacity buys nothing at all here.
