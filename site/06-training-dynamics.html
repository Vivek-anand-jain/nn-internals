Training Dynamics: The Run, Not The Step

Everything so far has been one iteration, taken apart. A real run is a hundred thousand of them, and nothing that goes wrong in training goes wrong inside a step — it goes wrong across them. This page trains the same 13-parameter model, over and over, and measures the five things that only exist over a run: the learning-rate schedule, the initialisation, the clip, the spike, and the decay.
