Batching: the scheduler is the throughput

A decode step reads every weight in the model to produce one token, so a second sequence riding along in the same step is nearly free. That single fact turns serving into a scheduling problem: what matters is not how fast one step is but how many sequences are in flight during it, and therefore when a finished sequence gives its slot back and what is allowed to block a step. This page simulates a queue of requests through two schedulers and one prefill policy, and counts the difference.
