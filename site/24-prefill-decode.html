Prefill vs Decode: One Machine, Two Regimes

Training had one organising idea: four classes of tensor, and where the bytes go. Inference has a different one, and everything in Part IV is an attack on it: arithmetic intensity — FLOPs performed per byte moved out of memory. Generating text runs the same weights in two completely different shapes. Prefill puts the whole prompt through at once and is a big matrix multiply. Decode puts one token through and is a matrix–vector product. Same weights, same bytes read, three orders of magnitude less arithmetic per byte. This page measures that gap and puts both regimes on a roofline.
