MoE Training

Experts do not fit on one GPU, so they are split across GPUs. Tokens arrive sharded by sequence, and the router — not the programmer — decides which GPU each one has to visit. That is an all‑to‑all, the one collective this site has taught and never used, and its payload size is not known until the router has run. Then there is the harder problem: nothing makes the router balanced.
