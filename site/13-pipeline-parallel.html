Pipeline Parallel

Tensor parallelism all-reduces activations at every layer, so it needs a fast link and stays inside one node. To go beyond a node you need a strategy that tolerates a slow one. Pipeline parallelism splits the model by depth and communicates only at layer boundaries: one activation tensor per boundary, point-to-point, no collectives. It is the cheapest communication of any strategy, which is exactly why it is the one that crosses nodes. The bill it pays is time — the bubble.
