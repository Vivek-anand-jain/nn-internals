Part 29

Speculative Decoding

Decode is bandwidth-bound: a forward pass costs what it costs to read the weights, and reading them for one token costs the same as reading them for eight. So let a small model guess the next few tokens, and have the big model check all of them in a single pass. The surprising part is not the speed — it is that the guessing changes nothing. The acceptance rule reproduces the big model's output distribution exactly, and this page proves it twice: once as an identity, once by simulating a hundred and twenty thousand rollouts against the enumerated truth.
