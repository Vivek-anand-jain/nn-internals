Part 31

Serving at Scale

A serving system is a scheduler. Everything the previous seven pages built — the KV cache, paged blocks, continuous batching, quantisation, speculation, the parallelism layout — is a knob it turns, and it turns them against each other, because time-to-first-token, inter-token latency and throughput cannot all be maximised at once. This page prices the tradeoff: what a prefill costs the decodes running beside it, what it costs to move a KV cache to a different machine instead, and what happens when the cache is full and something has to give.
