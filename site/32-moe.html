Part 32

Mixture of Experts

A dense MLP uses every weight for every token, which means parameter count and compute per token are the same question asked twice. A mixture of experts holds N separate MLPs and sends each token to only k of them. Capacity then grows with N while arithmetic grows with k, and the two numbers come apart. This page shows the router that makes the choice, the grouping that makes it fast, and the memory bill that arrives regardless.
