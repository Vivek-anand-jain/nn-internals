ZeRO & FSDP: Stop Replicating What You Don't Need

DDP replicates everything. Four GPUs hold four identical copies of the weights, the gradients and the optimizer state — three quarters of that is waste that buys nothing. ZeRO asks one question: which of these actually needs to be on every GPU? The answer, one class at a time, is the three stages. Nothing on this page changes a single number the model computes.
