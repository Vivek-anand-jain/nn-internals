Inference Parallelism

The same three axes as training — TP, PP, DP — and the tradeoffs invert. In training the batch is enormous and the step is long, so tensor parallelism's all-reduce amortises and overlaps, and the pipeline bubble is the thing you fight. In decode the batch is small and the step is a few milliseconds, so the all-reduce sits on the critical path of every single token, its payload is too small to be a bandwidth problem, and it is latency that refuses to amortise. This page recomputes page 19's and page 21's arithmetic for one token instead of one training step, and the conclusions land the other way up.
