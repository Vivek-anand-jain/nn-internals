Tensor Parallel: Cut the Matrix Itself

Every strategy so far has moved whole tensors between GPUs and left the arithmetic alone. Tensor parallelism splits the matrix multiply. No GPU ever holds the full weight matrix, no GPU ever holds the full activation, and the answer is still bit-for-bit the single-GPU answer. The price is an all-reduce that sits on the critical path and cannot be hidden.
