Part 28

Quantisation

A quantised weight is not a smaller float. It is an integer plus a scale, and every question that matters — per-tensor or per-channel, how big a group, INT4 or FP8, why the outliers ruin everything — is a question about which grid you snapped onto and what you chose to set its spacing. This page builds the grids and measures the damage. Then it shows the thing that makes quantisation an inference technique: the win lands almost entirely on decode, because decode is bandwidth-bound and prefill is not.
