The KV Cache: Exact Memoisation, and What It Costs

Attention at position n needs the keys and values of every position before it. Recomputing them all on every step makes generation quadratic. Caching them makes it linear — and the cache is not an approximation: K and V for a token depend only on that token's hidden state, and causality guarantees nothing later can change it. This page proves that equivalence in your browser, then prices the cache. At 128k context on a 70B model it is 42.95 GB per sequence, and without grouped-query attention it would be eight times that.
